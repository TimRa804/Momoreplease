Frequently Asked Questions

What is Momo Activity Box?

Momo Activity Box is a box of experiences designed to bring creativity, learning, and fun right to your home.

Each box includes ready-to-use worksheets, crafts, and all the materials needed to complete the activities. Just open the box and let the fun begin!

What ages are the activity boxes for?

Momo Activity Box is designed primarily for children ages 2–5, with parental guidance and participation.

Older children are welcome to enjoy Momo too — especially if they love crafts, creating, and hands-on activities!

Do I need to buy additional supplies?

No! Everything needed for the activities is included in your box.

✂️ Already have scissors, glue, and coloring tools? Perfect — use what you already have!

Don’t have them? You can add our Starter Tool Kit for $8.99, which includes the basic tools you’ll need.

Does my child need adult supervision?

Yes. Momo Activity Box is designed for children to enjoy with parental guidance, especially for younger children. Some activities may involve small pieces or tools that require adult assistance.

What if something is missing or damaged?

We’re here to help! Please contact us at momoreplease@gmail.com within 3 days of receiving your box and let us know what happened. We’ll do our best to make it right.

Can I return my Momo Activity Box?

Because our boxes contain curated activity materials prepared specifically for each experience, all Momo Activity Box sales are final and we do not accept returns.

If your box arrives damaged or something is missing, please contact us within 3 days of delivery at momoreplease@gmail.com so we can help.

Is Momo Activity Box educational?

Absolutely! Momo is designed to make learning feel like play. Activities encourage children to explore creativity, fine-motor skills, problem-solving, following directions, early learning, and independent thinking — all while having fun.

How often will I receive a box?

Each box is a one-time purchase.

Can I purchase a box as a gift?

Absolutely! Momo Activity Box makes a fun gift for birthdays, holidays, or simply because you want to give a child something fun to do.
